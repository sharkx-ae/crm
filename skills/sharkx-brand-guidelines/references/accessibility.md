# Accessibility — SHARKX palette

## Contrast (foreground on white `#FFFFFF`)

| Pair | Notes |
|------|--------|
| Black `#1c0f0f` on white | ✅ Primary body |
| Red `#e70101` on white | ✅ Large text / links; avoid long red paragraphs |
| White on Red `#e70101` | ✅ Primary buttons, title bands, table headers |
| White on Black `#1c0f0f` | ✅ Dark sections |
| Muted `#5c5656` on white | ✅ Captions if ≥14px |
| Faint `#a49f9f` on white | ❌ Not for body text |
| Success `#0a7c42` on white | ⚠️ Icons/fills only — use black label |

## Focus

HTML: `3px solid #e70101`, offset 2px.

## Charts

Direct labels or legend + title; do not rely on red vs black alone — add patterns or labels where needed.
