# Brand Book 2026 — summary (SHARKX)

Full PDF: `references/brand-book-sharkx-2026.pdf`

## Tagline & URL

- **Tagline:** Marketing Built to Dominate
- **Website:** sharkx.ae (lowercase **sharkx** only in URL context)

## Logo system

| Context | Rule |
|---------|------|
| Primary mark | White **SHARKX** on **red** background — brand-led, high-impact |
| Light backgrounds | Logo in **red** (primary) or **black** (corporate/supporting) |
| Dark backgrounds | Logo **white only** — no gradients or effects on static assets |
| Descriptor | **SHARKX Marketing** — only when marketing services must be named; never replaces primary on premium touchpoints |
| Icons | Primary: red bg + white “X”. Alternates: white bg + red X; black bg + white X. **Gradient icons: digital/motion only** — not print or static Office |
| Clear space | Exclusion zone — no text, graphics, imagery, or page edges inside minimum area |
| Minimum size | Digital **24px** height · Print **15mm** height |
| Misuse | No stretch, rotate, unapproved colors, effects, busy backgrounds, typography changes, redraws, or swapping primary for Marketing lockup |

## Color

| Name | HEX | Role |
|------|-----|------|
| SHARKX Red | `#e70101` | Primary — leads high-impact expressions |
| SHARKX Black | `#1c0f0f` | Secondary — structure, contrast, focus |

Brand book shows **10% / 40% / 60%** tints for each — implemented in `assets/tokens.json` as `redTint*` and `blackTint*`.

## Typography

| Role | Face | Weights |
|------|------|---------|
| Latin (primary) | Sharp Sans | Light · Medium · Bold |
| Hierarchy | H1 Bold · H2 Medium · Body Light/Regular · UI/Caption Light/Regular |
| Arabic (supporting) | GE Flow | Headlines Bold · Body/UI Regular |

**Rule:** No substitute typefaces without approval. Structure and readability over decoration.

## Voice

- Confident, not safe · Sharp, not verbose · Strategic, not decorative · Built for impact, not approval
- Short decisive statements · active voice · one idea per sentence · outcomes before claims
- **Brand rule:** If it could belong to any agency, it doesn’t belong to SHARKX.
